About this service

What this work involves

RJA lists street lighting for car parks and public areas, and describes access equipment including a cherry picker and scaffold towers. The first step is to identify the fittings, the site, and whether the work is a new installation, repair or maintenance visit. Traffic, working height and access windows may affect how the job is organised.

To make the first conversation useful

  • Provide the site location and number of fittings affected.
  • Describe the fault or proposed lighting change.
  • Mention vehicle movement, access restrictions and working height.

Can RJA reach elevated fittings?

RJA describes in-house access equipment. The team can confirm a suitable method after reviewing the site.

Service scope is based on RJA's published information. Confirm current availability and the exact scope with RJA.
