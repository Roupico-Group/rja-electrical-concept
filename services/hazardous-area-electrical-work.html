About this service

What this work involves

RJA lists hazardous area electrical design, installation and maintenance, including work for petrol station forecourts and the petroleum industry. This is specialist work, so a detailed enquiry should identify the facility, affected equipment, operating constraints and existing documentation. RJA can then confirm whether the team is suited to the requested scope and how it would be assessed.

To make the first conversation useful

  • Describe the facility and the specific equipment or area involved.
  • Share relevant drawings, classifications and maintenance records.
  • Explain access, shutdown constraints and the timing of the work.

Is this a general electrical callout?

It is a specialist site enquiry. Contact RJA directly with the relevant technical and site details before planning work.
