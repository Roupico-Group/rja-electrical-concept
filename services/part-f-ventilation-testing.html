About this service

What this work involves

RJA lists Part F ventilation testing as a specialist service. Ventilation issues can also sit alongside condensation concerns, so it helps to explain whether the request relates to new work, an existing system or a problem in use. RJA can confirm the measurement and reporting scope once the property and installed ventilation are known.

To make the first conversation useful

  • Describe the property and ventilation equipment.
  • Explain whether this is for building work, investigation or a follow-up test.
  • Share plans, commissioning information or earlier readings if available.

Can this help with condensation concerns?

Testing can inform a wider investigation, but the cause of condensation needs to be assessed in the context of the property.

Service scope is based on RJA's published information. Confirm current availability and the exact scope with RJA.
