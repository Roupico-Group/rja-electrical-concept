About this service

What this work involves

RJA lists inspection and testing across its domestic, commercial and industrial work. The scope may involve fixed wiring, electrical equipment or a particular safety concern, so the first step is to identify what needs to be assessed. For a business or managed building, it also helps to consider occupied areas and access to distribution equipment when planning a visit.

To make the first conversation useful

  • Describe the site and the installation or equipment to be checked.
  • Share any previous report, fault history or deadline that affects the work.
  • Ask which report or record will be provided for the agreed scope.

Is this the same as an EICR?

An EICR is one type of fixed installation inspection. RJA can confirm which inspection or test fits the enquiry.

Service scope is based on RJA's published information. Confirm current availability and the exact scope with RJA.
