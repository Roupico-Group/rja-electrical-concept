About this service

What this work involves

RJA lists electrical maintenance, fault finding and repairs for commercial settings, as well as responsive work. For an organisation, a maintenance enquiry should set out the premises, existing equipment, known problems and any access constraints. Urgent faults need the direct call route; planned work can be scoped with the appropriate testing, inspection and follow-up.

To make the first conversation useful

  • List recurring faults and areas where downtime matters.
  • Share previous inspection reports or maintenance records.
  • Separate urgent issues from work that can be scheduled.

What if the fault is urgent?

RJA advertises a 24-hour emergency electrician service. Use the emergency number for an urgent issue rather than a demo enquiry form.
