About this service

What this work involves

RJA advertises full and partial rewires alongside electrical installation work. A rewire might be considered during a renovation, after testing reveals concerns or when the existing layout no longer supports the property. The scope should be agreed before work begins, including rooms affected, fixtures, access, and the level of making good expected.

To make the first conversation useful

  • Describe the property, planned changes and any existing faults.
  • Share layouts or photos of the consumer unit and key rooms.
  • Ask how testing, certification and disruption will be handled.

Does every job require a full rewire?

No. RJA lists both full and partial rewires. The appropriate scope depends on the installation and the work being planned.
