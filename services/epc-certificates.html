About this service

What this work involves

RJA includes Energy Performance Certificates among its services and lists them alongside landlord and property work. The enquiry should establish the property type, address and reason the certificate is needed. A visit or assessment can then be arranged with suitable access and any useful existing property information.

To make the first conversation useful

  • Provide the full property address and its use.
  • Mention any deadline or earlier EPC reference.
  • Ask what access and documents the assessor will need.

Is an EPC the same as an electrical certificate?

No. An EPC concerns a property's energy performance; an electrical report examines the electrical installation. RJA lists both services.

Service scope is based on RJA's published information. Confirm current availability and the exact scope with RJA.
